🔬 Methodology

The Gene Expression Correlation score assesses each drug's potential to reverse the active disease transcriptomic signature using a Connectivity Map-inspired approach across 5 weighted dimensions:

  • Signature Reversal (35%) — Does the drug's mechanism reverse genes dysregulated in the active disease signature? Curated from published disease transcriptomic studies.
  • Target-Disease Overlap (25%) — How many of the drug's target genes directly overlap with dysregulated disease genes? Weighted by fold-change magnitude.
  • Cell Type Specificity (20%) — Is the drug active in cell types most relevant to the selected disease pathology?
  • Expression Evidence (15%) — How well-studied is the drug/gene in disease transcriptomic literature? Based on PubMed reference count.
  • Directionality (5%) — Is the drug's directional effect consistent with the active disease signature?
