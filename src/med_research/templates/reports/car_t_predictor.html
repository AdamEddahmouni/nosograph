🔬 Methodology

The CAR-T Response Predictor scores each {{ ctx_disease }}-associated gene for CD19 CAR-T therapy suitability across 5 weighted dimensions:

  • B Cell Dependency (35%) — How central is the gene to B cell biology? B cell-intrinsic genes (CD19, CD20, BLK, BTK, BANK1, PRDM1) score highest.

  • CD19 Targeting (15%) — Does CD19 CAR-T directly impact this pathway? CD19 itself, CD20, and BCR signaling genes score highest.
  • Clinical Evidence (5%) — Existing published CAR-T or deep B cell depletion data involving this pathway.
