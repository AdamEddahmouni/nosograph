🔬 Methodology

The CAR-T Response Predictor scores each lupus-associated gene for CD19 CAR-T therapy suitability across 5 weighted dimensions:

  • B Cell Dependency (35%) — How central is the gene to B cell biology? B cell-intrinsic genes (CD19, CD20, BLK, BTK, BANK1, PRDM1) score highest.
  • Autoantibody Association (25%) — How strongly linked to pathogenic autoantibodies (anti-dsDNA, ANA)? BAFF, PRDM1, CD40L top this dimension.
  • Plasma Cell Relevance (20%) — Is the gene critical for long-lived plasma cell survival? PRDM1 (BLIMP-1), IKZF1/3, UBE2L3 are key.
  • CD19 Targeting (15%) — Does CD19 CAR-T directly impact this pathway? CD19 itself, CD20, and BCR signaling genes score highest.
  • Clinical Evidence (5%) — Existing published CAR-T or deep B cell depletion data involving this pathway.

Clinical Context: CD19 CAR-T cell therapy (Erlangen protocol) achieved drug-free remission in severe refractory SLE by deep-depleting the entire CD19+ B lineage. Genes scoring ≥8.0 are in B-cell-intrinsic pathways likely to respond best. Genes <5.0 are in non-B-cell pathways where CAR-T alone may be insufficient.
