# Scientific inventor's notebook — visual system

**Director's selection:** Paper-like scientific, engineering and architectural drafting with very restrained botanical hints. Do not turn the design into an old botanical apothecary, generic parchment artifact, or neon science montage.

## Texture

Near-new, slightly fibrous ivory stock; almost imperceptible speckling; tonal paper shift measured in single-digit percent; clean margins; no harsh stains, heavy cracking, burned edges, arbitrary cursive or fake medieval aging.

## Diagram practices

Scientific illustration only where it serves understanding: cross-sections, surgical/anatomical mechanisms, microscopic structure, numerical models, kinematic mechanism sketches, measurement grids and geometry. Every real scientific diagram should be checked for correct anatomy, labels, scale, units and cited source.

Construction lines, compass arcs, leaders, dimensional ticks, small figure numbers and registration marks are allowed when they explain the image or indicate intentional structure.

## Design hierarchy

1. One meaningful central idea per frame.
2. NosoVolution emblem and editorial serif wordmark.
3. A clear research sentence or real data figure.
4. Small, readable technical sans-serif annotation.
5. Diagrams or measured technical ornament used with restraint.

## Colors

Midnight ink #102238; scientific slate blue #6288A3; paper #F5F1E8; graphite #49535B. Warm ochre/copper secondary only, and never as a fake scientific warning status.

## Video motion

Compass construction, pen tracing, measured assembly, faded charcoal ghost lines, tracing paper overlays, labeled technical zooms, mechanical pencil and page-turn textures. Prefer deterministic SVG or procedural motion for mathematical text and explanatory diagrams.

## Do not use

Glowing DNA stock renders, floating translucent medical bodies, inaccurate mathematical formulas, fake clinical screenshots, row after row of glowing cards, overpromising taglines, long small-print text in moving frames, or an always-visible cartoon companion.

## Application map

Public website introduction; research workbench; technical notes and data; launch film; Instagram launch tiles; professional presentations; conference badge/pin and printed researcher notebook.
