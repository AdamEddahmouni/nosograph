# Ovo — character identity program

**Name:** Ovo (visual variant `oVo` may be explored). **Status:** character and trademark clearance not approved; no final mascot design selected.

## Purpose

Create an unusually memorable, affectionate, marketable companion for NosoVolution that makes medical research approachable without implying clinical authority. It must support research onboarding, educational scenes, small interface moments, social media, physical plush and playful storytelling.

## Personality

Endlessly curious, self-aware about uncertainty, compassionate, persistent through failed experiments, funny through sincere fascination rather than forced corporate jokes. Ovo can say "I'm not sure yet" and can acknowledge errors.

## Design corrections from director

Several generated examples of giant glossy eyes, glowing fins, rabbit ears, halos, cuddly fantasy fur, glowing chest symbols and science-themed accessories were rejected as generic "AI mascot" artwork. Do **not** assume those designs are approved.

A promising structural hypothesis is a distinctive asymmetrical living fold or a character whose **whole** body has oVo-like form; even that was not finalized. Prioritize analog character design, silhouette-only studies, acting and pose sheets, anatomical consistency, pencil/ink sketches and a clear physical plush construction before 3D polish.

## Relationship to brand

The company mark remains a restrained scientific identity. Ovo can be a humorous, warm counterpart. Reuse a subtle geometric relationship only when it improves recognizability; do not glue the corporate logo to the character's belly.

## Research-UI boundaries

Ovo may aid navigation, explain interface concepts and celebrate verified tasks. It must not claim diagnoses, treatments, medical certainty or expert review. Allow hide/reduced-motion/off. When discussing clinical-style evidence, explicitly distinguish uncertain results.

## Required selection gates

Originality research; three silhouette families; user-selected final anatomy; front/side/back and dynamic expression reference; limited color/monochrome studies; consistency at icon scale; textile-pattern prototype; accessibility evaluation; commercial rights and name clearance before merchandise.
