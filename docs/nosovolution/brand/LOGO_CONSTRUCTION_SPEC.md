# oVo symbol — technical construction specification (concept)

The original selected board uses a merged `oVo` structure: rounded bilateral circular forms turning inward to a central V, a very restrained small upward form, and optional two vertically separated dots beneath the V. The intended symbolism is observation, connected domains, convergence and knowledge evolution. It is **not** a medical assurance seal or a registered mark.

## Drawing guidelines

1. Draw a centerline x=0, a shared baseline, two visually balanced circular envelopes and a V whose valley sits on the central axis.
2. Preserve an open/cupped negative space at the inward portions of the o-shapes so the emblem does not collapse into a generic infinity symbol.
3. Use optical adjustments (not only geometric symmetry) to balance left/right mass and the convergence point.
4. Keep the upward motif abstract and very small; if it starts reading as a floral logo, simplify or remove it. Technical engineering diagrams take priority over plant illustration.
5. Keep the two knowledge-trail dots as an optional full-size lockup. Omit them from small-size marks if they resemble punctuation or merge together.
6. Never require gradients, glow, textures, or paper in the actual vector: those belong to the surrounding brand system.
7. Maintain separate versions for 16px, 24px, 32px, large signage, dark background, light background and pure monochrome.
8. Build the final wordmark with licensed type; check all kerning pairs and the distinctive capital V transition.

## Approved atmosphere

Subtly fibrous **near-new** ivory paper, muted scientific blue and deep navy, thin measured rules, instrument marks, drafting annotations, anatomical sections, mechanical cross-sections, and meticulously verified equations. Leonardo da Vinci is an inspiration for curiosity, experiment, anatomy and engineering notebooks—not a source of paintings or an excuse for fake ancient scripts.

## Validation

Silhouette recognition, anti-aliasing, high-contrast proof, originality check, prior visual marks, trademark clearance, mobile app icon scaling, accessibility, SVG metadata and export QA.

**Status:** chosen composition and style; vectors in this repository are provisional reconstructions, not a final hand-refined registered identity.
