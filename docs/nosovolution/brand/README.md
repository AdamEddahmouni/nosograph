# NosoVolution brand — selected direction, not final trademark artwork

**Visual direction:** An architectural-scientific notebook translated into modern research software.

### Symbol

A merged **oVo** mark: the left and right circular paths represent connected domains; the central V forms a discriminating research question or meeting point. A small ascending blue shape is a restrained nod to life/scientific growth; optional lower dots represent a trace of inquiry. Both purely geometric and growth-accent versions need comparison.

The user selected this design direction from a concept board. The SVGs alongside this note are **new editable working reconstructions**, not pixel-accurate trace work or final cleared logos. Replace only after design and originality review.

### Palette (provisional)

- Ink / midnight navy: `#102238`
- Scientific slate blue: `#6288A3`
- Ivory paper: `#F5F1E8`
- Drafting graphite: `#49535B`
- Copper annotation sparingly: `#8B7355`

### Graphic grammar

- Lightly fibrous, clean archival paper: not tea-stained, heavily cracked or antique.
- Mechanical drafting lines: compasses, diameters, register marks, detail callouts, hatching, annotated sections.
- Biomedical drawings should be scientifically meaningful, sourced and checked.
- Leonardo da Vinci inspiration is **investigative process** (anatomy, engineering, measurement, iteration), not a copy of the Vitruvian Man or other artworks.
- Serif editorial headlines; restrained functional sans-serif for APIs, labels and tables.
- Do not use glowing generic DNA, fake research dashboards, fantasy medical anatomy, or inaccurate decorative equations.
- Mascot Ovo is **still in character-development**, not a finished company symbol.
