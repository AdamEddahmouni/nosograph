## Creative principle

Recreate the **reference's editorial rhythm**, not its original copy or exact graphics: identity construction → research problem → traceable evidence → scientific qualification → scope → software proof → open invitation. Use the approved oVo mark and scientific inventor's notebook visual system: nearly-new warm ivory paper, midnight navy ink, muted blue, precise anatomical/engineering linework, functional callouts, no decorative science fiction.

**IMPORTANT PRODUCT TRUTH:** The existing public software is the NosoGraph v0.2.1 public-alpha open-source foundation. NosoVolution is the approved future-facing name and broader research program. Research-model and simulation ecosystem features remain planned. The existing repository is `AdamEddahmouni/nosograph` unless renamed after verification. A film should never imply that roadmap concepts have shipped.
