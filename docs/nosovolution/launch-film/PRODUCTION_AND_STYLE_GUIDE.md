# NosoVolution — Film production direction

## Aesthetic: a scientist–inventor's living notebook
- Paper: `#F5F1E8`, subtle handmade fibers and almost imperceptible age; approximately 3–5% mottling, no distressed edges, coffee stains or antique sepia wash.
- Ink: navy `#102238` for primary typography, desaturated research blue `#6288A3` for traced mechanisms, charcoal `#49535B` for scientific annotations. Use only one active accent at a time.
- Typography: refined editorial serif (Fraunces or Source Serif 4) for headline statements and wordmark reference; Inter or equivalent for factual labels/UI. Final logotype is custom and must come from the approved design master.
- Drawing language: graphite compass arcs, 0.5–1.0px construction lines, ruler ticks, annotated tissue cross-sections, instrument sketches, measured technical elevations, equations with units and stated variables.
- Leonardo reference: methods of observation, dissection, measurement, diagramming and iterating design. Not literal recreations of the Vitruvian Man or a collage of famous artworks.
- On-screen copy: 8–13 words per scene maximum except sourced data/labels. Do not mimic a fake Renaissance manuscript in the main interface; keep it legible and modern.
- Screen-readability: screen copy at least 60px at 1920×1080 for hero lines; 34px for prominent labels; tiny figure annotations may be smaller and decorative but never hold essential claims.
- Movement: restrained 3–10px parallax, 8–14-frame ink strokes, natural eases, no dynamic lens flares/particles, no invented holograms.
- Audio: Original understated piano/felt-texture score, fine paper pencil sounds, subtle low strings near 27–36s. Licensed or self-composed only; do not copy uploaded reference audio without rights.

## Shot-specific scientific rules
- Evidence graph must show `Disease → Claim → Evidence relationship → Study / source → Provenance` correctly.
- Supports/contradicts/inconclusive/unasserted lanes describe claim evidence direction, not an automatic quality score.
- Registry count 10,404 is a published 2026-08-22 public-alpha snapshot, not today's independently regenerated count. Show qualification or omit number.
- Simulations, experimental planning and multiscale labs are **aspirations**; explicitly label vision / in development.
- Product visuals must come from actual repository-based app capture, or bear a visible **concept** label.

## Edit and deliveries
- Master: H.264 MP4 1920×1080 30fps, duration 40s, audio stereo 48kHz; source preferred ProRes 422/H.264 high quality. Export separate soundtrack, captions, and a no-VO music-led master.
- Vertical: recompose all scenes individually for 1080×1920 safe areas; never naively crop the 16:9 master.
- 4:5: 1080×1350 with logos and numeric microcopy sized for social.
- Accessibility: subtitles for any VO; contrast and reading durations verified on phone; if video autoplays muted, the on-screen copy stands alone.
- Do not present synthetic screenshots, invented metrics, patient information, clinical claims, or implied hosted services.
