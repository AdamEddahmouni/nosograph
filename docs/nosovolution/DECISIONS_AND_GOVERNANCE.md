# Approved decisions and open gates

## Locked / selected

1. **Project name: NosoVolution** — explicitly approved. Existing repository and package remain NosoGraph until a compatible migration.
2. **Identity direction:** An abstract merged **o–V–o** symbol; two rounded loops converging in the central V, delicate blue growth/observation motif, optional small lower dots. Refine symbol geometry for small sizes.
3. **Art direction:** Deep midnight navy, muted scientific blue, very light ivory drafting-paper texture; modern editorial typography; scientific/engineering/medical/architectural notebook diagrams inspired by Leonardo da Vinci's investigative methods, not his paintings or iconic copied figures. Paper should look lightly fibrous, **not antique or heavily distressed**.
4. **Floral restraint:** Minimal; mechanical, scientific and architectural drawing language takes precedence over botanical decoration.
5. **Mascot name proposal:** Ovo / oVo. The name is promising but mascot anatomy/artwork is **not finalized**. It should become a distinct lovable character, not another neon-eyed fantasy creature. Naming/IP clearance pending.
6. **Public launch intent:** User requested launch materials for a 40-second video and Instagram. Draft film script is approved as a working brief, not a rendered final video.
7. **Science:** Incremental, source-grounded, reproducible, skeptical research architecture; distinguish code maturity from scientific and clinical validity.
8. **Cost and governance:** Prefer free, local-first technology, independent verification, no chargeable services or publication without explicit approval.
