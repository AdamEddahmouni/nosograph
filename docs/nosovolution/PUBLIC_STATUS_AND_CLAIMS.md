**Known scientific weakness requiring correction:** evidence-quality implementation uses heuristics that may assign statistical quality from p-value / confidence-interval words alone. This is not a validated risk-of-bias or certainty appraisal.

**Public-release rule:** The launch film may show genuine NosoGraph screenshots and documented alpha features. Future NosoVolution modeling, simulations, and experiments must be explicitly labeled **VISION / IN DEVELOPMENT**. Annotate historical catalog numbers with snapshot dates and caveats. Do not publish a false live demo or equate a planning catalog with shipped modules.

**Rights:** Do not publish reference videos, third-party music, controlled data, private medical records, personal photos, paid source assets, or generative assets with unclear usage rights. Verify every external dataset/source license and citation.

**Clinical boundary:** Research use only; not a medical device, clinical decision support or treatment recommendation product.
